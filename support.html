FREQUENTLY ASKED
How do I verify an item is authentic?
Scan the QR code or tag on your item, or search its code on the verification page. Every genuine VALENCE item resolves to a product page showing its edition, batch, and registered owner (if claimed).
How do pre-orders and deposits work?
Pre-ordering an open drop requires a 50% deposit, paid securely through Paystack, to confirm your spot. The remaining balance is due before your item ships.
Where's my order?
Every purchase gets a tracking number. Check its status anytime under Orders in the sidebar — it moves through Paid, Processing, Shipped, and Delivered.
Can I change my registered item's owner?
Ownership transfers aren't self-service yet. Message support with the item's serial number and details of the new owner and we'll help.
